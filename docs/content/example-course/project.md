# Job Board

Build a small **job board** where companies post jobs and students apply.

## Must have

1. A home page that lists open jobs (title, company, location, type).
2. A job detail page with an **Apply** form (name, email, link to resume).
3. A simple **Post a job** form.
4. Data is stored in a database (MongoDB) through your own Express API.
5. The site is **deployed** and works on a phone.

## Submit

- Your **GitHub repository** link (public).
- The **live link** of the deployed site.
- A short note: what you built, what was hardest, what you'd add next.
